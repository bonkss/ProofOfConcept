Vietnamese Affogato

🥕🌽🍅What You Will Need 🥕🌽🍅

TOOLS

  • Vietnamese Coffee Press
  • Cup
  • Any Heating Element for Water

INGRIDIENTS

  • Hot Water
  • Vanilla Ice Cream
  • Cafe Du Monde

🔧☕️INSTRUCTIONS☕️🔧

  1. Begin boiling water
  2. Place the Coffee Press's Spanner on top of the cup of water
  3. Place the Filter Chamber on top of the Spanner
  4. Add 2 spoons of Coffee De Monde into the Chamber
  5. Place Filter Press on top of the Cafe Du Monde
  6. Add boiling water to the top of the filter press
  7. Fill to the top and cover the water with the Cap
  8. Allow the coffee to drip down until no water is left
  9. Once the dripping is complete, add a scoop of vanilla
  10. Enjoy!
